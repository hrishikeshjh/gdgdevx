# Hackrit — Full Site Copy Document

Voice check for every line below: plain verb, real number, no adjective that isn't information. Sections marked "KEEP AS-IS" are already doing this well — don't touch them just to touch them.

---

## 1. Hero

```
24-HOUR HACKATHON OF DEVX 2.0
ORGANISED BY GDG ON CAMPUS TIU

REGISTER NOW →        DEVX 2.0 →
```

If you keep a clock element, label it depending on what it's showing:

- **Duration display:** small label above it — `DURATION` — then `24:00`
- **Live countdown to kickoff:** small label above it — `STARTS IN` — then real ticking `DD : HH : MM`

Pick one meaning, not both — right now it's ambiguous which one it is.

## 3. About Hackrit — four cards

**Problem:** the current four cards (Skilled Builders / Product Demos / High Visibility / Mentorship) are written from a sponsor's point of view — "connect with," "brand positioning," "hiring partners." A participant reading the About section doesn't care what a sponsor gets.

**Fix: two separate card sets.**

### New participant-facing cards (replace the current ones in About)
```
WHAT YOU BUILD    One prototype, built and demoed in 24 hours —
                  not a slide deck.

WHO YOU MEET      Mentors, judges, and 2,500+ builders across
                  six domains.

WHAT YOU WIN      Cash prizes, cloud credits, and direct
                  interview fast-tracks.

WHAT YOU GET      Certificate, swag, and a working project for
                  your portfolio.
```

### Relocate the old cards, unchanged, into a Sponsors section
```
SKILLED BUILDERS   Connect with passionate student hackers
                   creating impactful real-world software &
                   systems.

PRODUCT DEMOS      Showcase cutting-edge developer platforms,
                   APIs, and modern toolchains directly to
                   builders.

HIGH VISIBILITY    Prominent brand positioning on-stage, across
                   official merchandise, and digital broadcasts.

MENTORSHIP         Direct engagement with technical leaders,
                   workshop conductors, and hiring partners.
```
These were good copy — just aimed at the wrong reader. They belong on a "Partner With Hackrit" / Sponsors page, not in About.
